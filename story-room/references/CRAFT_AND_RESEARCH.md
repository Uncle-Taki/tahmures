# Research lens and its limits

Use this reference for *analysis*, not as authority to rewrite Tahmures canon or to copy a playwright's style.

## Research-supported general principles
1. Saeed Talajooy, **Bahram Beyzaie's Dramatic and Cinematic Oeuvre** (Cinema Iranica): theatrical forms including ta'ziyeh, naqqali, taqlid, puppet and shadow plays; framing and reframing; historical/mythic story used to examine contemporary power, social roles and belonging; rich yet performable poetic register. https://cinema.iranicaonline.org/article/bahram-bayzais-dramatic-and-cinematic-oeuvre/
2. Talajooy, **Reformulation of Shahnameh Legends in Bahram Beyzaie's Plays** (Iranian Studies 46, 2013): reimagining older narrative structures; peripheral voices challenge central authority. https://doi.org/10.1080/00210862.2013.789735
3. Cambridge University Press, **Bahram Beyzaie (1938–2025)**: historical/legendary reinterpretation, questions of truth and accountability, masks and performance traditions. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/iranian-studies/article/bahram-beyzaie-19382025/5BC7603FE62419D4BB404A2124947EC2
4. University of St Andrews research portal, **The Plays and Films of Bahram Beyzaie** (2024): mythical/historical identity, genre, multilingual performance, agency of women, children, minorities, intellectuals. https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/the-plays-and-films-of-bahram-beyzaie-origins-forms-and-functions/
5. Writers Guild Foundation, **Screenplay Primers**: character voice, subtext, stakes, suspense, exposition, dialogue and inciting incidents as screenplay craft questions. https://www.wgfoundation.org/screenplay-primers
6. Laney College / LibreTexts, **Write a Scene with Dramatic Arc**: identify immediate objective, obstacle, emotional event and stakes; write only what can be seen or heard. https://human.libretexts.org/Courses/Laney_College/Scriptwriting_for_Video_Broadcast_and_Digital_Media_%28Zeigler%29/10%3A_The_Anatomy_of_a_Scene/10.03%3A_WORKSHEET-_WRITE_A_SCENE_WITH_DRAMATIC_ARC

## Interpretation — design choices for Tahmures, not direct quotes from the sources
- Give competing testimony a material consequence: a changed choice, power position or cost.
- Allow rituals to expose conflict through action and participants' status, not decorative historical ambience.
- Make the poetic/high-register line **playable** as tactical speech; juxtapose it with ordinary counter-speech or silence.
- Use meaningful props and repeated motifs to connect shots without crowding them with dialogue.
- Keep oral and theatrical tradition as creative opportunities, not mandatory formulas or a license for unsupported historical assertions.

## Originality guard
Generate fresh dialogue, images, structures, and characters within user's existing canon. Do not emulate the distinctive phrasing of Bahram Beyzai, reproduce/translate his lines, or copy any named scene/plot from his works. Label proposed art decisions as proposed, not historical canon. Do not write pseudo-archaic Persian to imply authenticity without native review.
