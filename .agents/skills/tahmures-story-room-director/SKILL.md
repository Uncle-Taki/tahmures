# Story-room director — production orchestrator

Use this when asked to **make Chapter 01 or another episode dramatically stronger**, to prepare writing for the Google Flow pipeline, or to run a complete story-room pass. Delegate the focused work to the skills below (read their actual files); do not duplicate all their methods here:

1. `$tahmures-episode-purpose` — episode promise, teaching bridge and audience transformation.
2. `$tahmures-character-pressure` — motive, opposition, agency and distinct voices.
3. `$tahmures-causal-architecture` — setup, reversal, irrevocable consequence, failure ownership.
4. `$tahmures-original-iranian-dramaturgy` — original Iranian myth/ritual/testimony dramaturgy, not phrase imitation.
5. `$tahmures-eleven-scene-blueprint` — scene changes and transitions, grounded to timeline.
6. `$tahmures-punch-and-timing` — suspense, reveals, reversals, costs, silence and landing moments.
7. `$tahmures-dialogue-and-subtext` — actor-playable exchanges and character voice.
8. `$tahmures-visual-sound-storytelling` — image/action and sonic meaning, 2D feasibility.
9. `$tahmures-series-continuity` — continuity, adaptation and source authority audit.
10. `$tahmures-story-red-team` — adversarial critique, ranked improvements and re-review.
11. `$tahmures-flow-handoff-sync` — rebuild the COMPLETE candidate Google Flow handoff after revised story and dialogue, with impact matrix and approval-based promotion gate.

## Non-negotiable source and approval rules
- Work inside the actual Tahmures repo. Read root/nested `AGENTS.md`, `meta-prompt.md`, `book/project-charter.md`, `book/narrative-continuity.md`, and the canonical English chapter relevant to the task. Follow project-local editorial skill and approvals. The English narrative and continuity are canonical for story facts; media adaptations have their own scoped approval.
- For Chapter 01, inspect `editorial/media-handoff/chapter-01-animation-status.json` and the selected revision under `Characters/animations/chapter-01-candidate-01/production/english-final-03/`, including its README, timeline and reviews, and check for documented superseding decisions.
- Do not assume an 11-scene count or change approved duration: verify the real timeline and flag any mismatch. Div-e Sefid serves as messenger in the approved Chapter 01 animation, while Chapar is a distinct courier. Do not merge them.
- Separate every claim or suggestion with labels **CANON**, **APPROVED MEDIA ADAPTATION**, **PROPOSAL**, **CONFLICT**, or **UNKNOWN**. Cite repo paths (and line/section/scene identifiers when available) for source-derived claims. A proposed line or scene is never an approved line or scene.
- Do not modify canonical English/Persian manuscripts, continuity, approved media, approvals, or status records without the owner's explicit permission. Do not create new voice, imagery, provider calls, uploads, commits, pushes, or deployments. Candidate writing stays in a new working folder.
- The episode must remain original teaching fiction distinct from *Shahnameh*: no modern engineering jargon in the ancient-world scene, a concrete chapter-specific failure with consequences, divs as capable and uncertain rather than just evil, a correctly identified failure owner, an explicit engineering bridge, and a hook to the next chapter.

## Operating model
- **Round A / discovery:** Read source hierarchy and approval/timeline status. Create `00_SOURCE_LEDGER.md` recording actual files, canonical vs scoped media, selected English chapter, current scene count, actual run time, approvals, unresolved questions. This is not an invitation to rewrite canon.
- **Round B / diagnosis:** Run skills 1–4 in sequence. Create a concise diagnosis before rewriting. For art-influenced craft use general dramaturgical devices, not close imitation of Beyzai.
- **Round C / construction:** Run 5–8 and produce actual **PROPOSAL** scene cards, punch map, dialogue candidates, storyboard/sound notes. Prefer strongest 1–3 surgical improvements when approved production already exists.
- **Round D / challenge:** Run 9–10; fix candidates not canon. Re-evaluate causality, purpose, timeline, voices, bridge and 2D feasibility until no unreported high-severity issues. Do not hide approval blockers.
- **Round E / synchronized handoff:** Produce `11_OWNER_DECISIONS.md`, `12_FLOW_WRITING_HANDOFF.md` and a candidate screenplay in the requested language. **Invoke `$tahmures-flow-handoff-sync` in this same run** to create the complete updated candidate Flow handoff (context, agent instructions, scene/shot prompts, reference maps, timing/audio maps, operator guides, change-impact matrix, verification and promotion plan). Handoff maps scene IDs to shot-level writing notes and references only authorized assets; missing approvals are blockers. Codex does not call Flow and does not generate videos, images or voices.

## Files and boundaries
- Write new results under `editorial/media-handoff/story-room/chapter-01-candidate-01/` unless the repository has an existing preferred **candidate** working location. Never overwrite an approved `english-final-03` file or the existing official Flow handoff. A separate `flow-handoff-candidate/` is mandatory for changed story work.
- Use templates in `story-room/templates/` if the package's local path is available; otherwise inspect their installed copies in the repo. Keep traceable pointers to sources and a change log.
- Do not treat 11 scenes as verified until actual timeline is read. Never rename existing scoped statuses. Do not mutate English or Persian manuscripts, approved audio, Godot exports, hashes, approval records, or `book/narrative-continuity.md` without explicit permission.
- Preserve optional variants with distinct `PROPOSAL` labels and owner-selectable tradeoffs. Offer no more than two complete alternate dialogue passes per pivotal exchange.

## End result
Provide numbered creation paths, count of actual scenes / candidate scene slots, pre/post quality concerns, top three prioritized dramatic upgrades, unresolved approval decisions, verification actually performed, and a clear statement that no provider calls, uploads, commits, pushes, or modifications of canon occurred.
