# Direct Shahnameh evidence for Tahmures

Tier A means explicit in the relevant *Shahnameh* verses, not merely repeated
in the research dossier. The project's fictional chapters remain Tier C even
when they use a Tier A person or motif.

Primary text: Ferdowsi, [Tahmures episode](https://ganjoor.net/ferdousi/shahname/tahmoores/sh1/)
and [opening of Jamshid's reign](https://ganjoor.net/ferdousi/shahname/jamshid/sh1/).
Research input: `.zcode/tahmures_characters.md`, especially sections 1–7.
Check verse text, not the host site's generated prose summaries, before
asserting a literary fact. Exact spelling/transliteration follows project
glossary and source context.

Tier A motif palette: a heavy mace; div-binding; the divs teaching nearly thirty
scripts; spinning wool into coverings; animals and birds brought into human
service; Shahrasp's counsel; divine glory and righteous rule. Use only where a
chapter earns the motif. The poem presents most civilizing arts **before** the
div battle; it specifically links writing to the spared divs. Do not repeat the
dossier's broader implication that all crafts were won from them.

Chronology from the verses: Hushang's reign precedes Tahmures; Tahmures's
crafts and Shahrasp's counsel precede the described div rebellion; surviving
divs teach writing afterward; the reign ends; Jamshid succeeds him. The poem
does not specify the ages or daily court roles of Hushang and Jamshid during
Tahmures's reign. Do not turn gaps into Tier A facts.

Name-collision caution: [Ferdowsi's later Zahhak episode](https://ganjoor.net/ferdousi/shahname/zahak/sh1/)
names Arnavaz as Jamshid's daughter. The book's Chapter 11 currently names a
garden keeper Arnavaz without stating that identity. This is a potential
genealogy/reader-confusion issue for a future scoped migration decision, not
permission to edit the frozen manuscript now.
