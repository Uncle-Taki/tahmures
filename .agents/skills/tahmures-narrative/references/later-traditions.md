# Later traditions and research leads

`TIER_B_LATER_TRADITION` is **not Shahnameh canon**. Attribute it by source and
do not insert it into the book as a fact of Ferdowsi's Tahmures episode.
Research input: `.zcode/tahmures_characters.md`, especially sections 2–3, 6,
8, and 10–11. The dossier mixes primary verse, later reports, and creative
ideas, so its labels require independent checking before a publication claim.

| Element | Status | Safe handling |
|---|---|---|
| Merv / مرو builder association | Later legendary attribution, absent from the Tahmures episode; a [scholarly reference](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/merv#2-mimari) reports competing medieval attributions for an early citadel's builder | May inspire a labelled later-tradition localization; do not call it Ferdowsi's location or establish the city's historical founder |
| Shiraz / شیراز founder association | Dossier reports a later attribution but supplies weak support; not in the Tahmures episode | Research the underlying source before factual use; otherwise use only as an unasserted creative lead |
| Avestan/Zoroastrian Jamshid relationship variants | Separate tradition from Ferdowsi's father–son sequence | Do not merge genealogies in one tale without a deliberate, documented project choice |
| Ahriman-riding and later death story variants | The *Shahnameh* itself depicts the binding/ride; later Rivayat material expands the death story; see [Iranica's tradition discussion](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/jamsid-i/) | Keep the direct verse and later elaboration separate; do not supply a death narrative as Ferdowsi's account |
| Additional spouse/children and city eponyms | Dossier-only or weakly traced leads | Do not add to the core cast without source and chronology review |
