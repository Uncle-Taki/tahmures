## Competing defensible arguments

[PROPOSAL; interpretation] Messenger: if the warning is current, waiting could be costly; deliver promptly to the present captain. Keeper: if the hand belongs to an earlier captain, preserve it as a record. Literal reader: the only definite requested act on the object is a count; execute that act accurately. Ruler: consulting three skilled readers seems a reasonable way to identify an object. Each argument protects a value and omits the shared criterion that the hall needed to decide first.

[CANON] The failure owner is requirements and interface design, not “bad divs”. [PROPOSAL] Preserve the tension between reasonable intent and unauthorized assumption. No testimony may resolve dissolved provenance by confidence or rank.
