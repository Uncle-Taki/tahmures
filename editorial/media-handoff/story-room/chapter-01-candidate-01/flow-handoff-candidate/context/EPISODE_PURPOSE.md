## Six different questions

- **External want [CANON]:** Tahmures wants the hall to classify the river tablet and state what is missing. His first request fails to specify that result; the servants pursue three different useful-looking tasks.
- **Dramatic question [PROPOSAL]:** Can a ruler recall the consequences of his own question without making those who answered it carry his blame?
- **Inner need / contradiction [PROPOSAL; inference]:** Tahmures must exchange the appearance of effortless command for the vulnerability of stating what he does not know. The source shows him stopping the reed; it does not diagnose a childhood wound or private trauma.
- **Two defensible values [PROPOSAL; interpretation]:** Acting before an urgent warning becomes useless versus refusing to give an unidentified record present authority. Careful cataloguing, prompt delivery and literal execution each protect something real, but none supplies the missing shared purpose.
- **Viewer emotional journey [PROPOSAL]:** Ease within a capable hall → unease at three incompatible certainties → concern for the soaked Black Div and frightened villages → relief at a correction that owns its origin → renewed uncertainty at the damp treaty. Do not invite laughter at the wet div.
- **Engineering transfer [CANON]:** An unspecified classification request lets data compete with instructions; real journeys and false public alarm follow; a separately stated task and explicit missing-context fields make the interaction inspectable. Requirements and interface design own the failure. Labels alone do not authenticate a source or enforce permission.

## Episode promise

[PROPOSAL — RECOMMENDED] When an unidentified tablet sends three capable readers in different directions, Tahmures must expose the missing purpose in his own request and recall an alarm his hall has spread, or leave frightened villages answering a command nobody can place; his choice tests whether authority includes the duty to correct itself.

[CANON] The cost is not a fabricated battle: a soaked return, riders sent into the hills and three villages believing invasion has begun. Time spent and the alarm already experienced cannot be undone. [PROPOSAL; inference] Public confidence may be strained; do not portray lost trust as a measured source fact.
