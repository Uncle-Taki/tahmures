## Restrained original option — RECOMMENDED PROPOSAL

[PROPOSAL] S02: Chapar completes the handoff, then holds the hand that might have indicated a sender empty. Do not mime a known route or conceal a recovered seal. Keep the existing narrator's uncertainty intact. S05: after the five approved lines, Tahmures looks from the wet hem to the tablet before the proposed acknowledgement, “I had not reckoned on the crossing.” The physical cost motivates the line; it is not a lecture on governance. S07: Akvan answers the proposed verbosity with “More words. The same question.” The king's changed action carries the reply's force. S10: the correction's local text overlay identifies the hall as the alarm's origin. S11: a missing middle becomes a too-fluent completion, with the unanswered question left for Chapter02.

[PROPOSAL] Performability: short breath units, distinct tactics, no ornamental historical diction. Staging uses already grounded tablet, reed, card and wet clothing. ## Ceremonially heightened original option — NOT selected

[PROPOSAL] Keep the same witnesses and actions; do not invent priests, masks, crowds or an attested court ceremony. Let the king's request be written and read back within the hall before it is released; the public record becomes an obstacle to saving face. Candidate Akvan line: “Your words have grown, my king. Your question has not.” A later correction is turned toward the same listeners rather than hidden as a private repair. This is a newly proposed performance frame, not a traditional Iranian rite or existing episode event.

[PROPOSAL] The stronger public exposure might intensify accountability but would add read-back burden, reaction coverage and new timing. It can overstate a modest interpretive failure as a grand tribunal, give every character ceremonial diction, and make the ruler's remorse displace the villagers' real cost. Retain as an owner-selectable tonal option; do not combine it with the economical line in one coherent shot package.
