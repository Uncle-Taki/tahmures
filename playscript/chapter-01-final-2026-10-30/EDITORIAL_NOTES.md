## Substantive changes to review

1. **PROPOSAL — accountability rather than ridicule.** Removed “دیوانه‌خانه” as the king’s explanation. Shahrasp challenges scope before the failure; the king defers the question, then acknowledges that he never chose the requested task. Each div remains competent within a different interpretation. Arash’s precaution is intelligible but rests on an unchecked inference of current authority.
2. **PROPOSAL — knowledge is not magically recovered.** Replaced “بازیابی اطلاعات گمشده” as the immediate goal with classification plus a separate account of knowns and unknowns. Simurgh supplies questions, not an authenticated author, recipient or date. The three question marks remain at the ending. This corrects a technical distinction, not merely style.
3. **PROPOSAL — one tablet and explicit custody.** Chapar brings one tablet, Shahrasp carries it to the Divan House, Div-e Sefid carries it to Arash, Arash explicitly returns it, and Div-e Sefid puts it on the hall table in S07. The lesson uses a separate sheet. The correction is a fresh sealed message; the ancient tablet stays at court.
4. **PROPOSAL — demonstrated cost.** One market closes because people interpret patrol departure and gate closure as evidence of war. Ordinary charcoal burners are shown to the audience before the court learns what Black Div saw. Eleven is a count of fires/kilns; their identification as watchfires is the mistaken assumption. No invasion, death, burned village or nationwide shutdown is invented.
5. **PROPOSAL — risk containment before a lesson.** After hearing the reports, the king sends a brief interim message against confrontation; he does not leave an active border scare unattended while listening to an extended explanation. The later correction names the recipient, reports known facts, recalls riders, corrects the rumour and requests both receipt and outcome.
6. **PROPOSAL — Hushang remains unverified.** A visualised association is expressly a mental image. The dialogue retains “resembles”, never “Hushang wrote this”. No speaking ghost, authenticated handwriting finding or revived king is added.
7. **OWNER DIRECTION / scoped adaptation:** Simurgh is summoned by the burning feather and appears only as a bird. This differs from the prior production’s indoor-human form policy; that policy is preserved in historical files rather than silently carried into this new owner-directed branch.
8. **PROPOSAL — short next-chapter link.** The final damp-treaty close-up is sourced as a motif from canonical “The Next Tale” but is an addition to this author’s source ending. It remains explicitly optional and unapproved. It does not import the old div role arrangement, spoken promises or invented missing text.
