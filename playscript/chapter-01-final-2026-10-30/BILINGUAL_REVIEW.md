S08-B005 tries a wordier remedy; S08-B008–B012 replaces that remedy with explicit roles and a requested result. Tahmures moves from authority to accountability. Simurgh’s questions direct attention rather than supplying unexplained factual knowledge.

Hushang’s mental image establishes resemblance, not verified authorship. The source ending is distinguished from the optional damp-treaty hook.
