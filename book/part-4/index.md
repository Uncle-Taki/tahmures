# Part IV — Evaluation, Security, and Production

Part IV completes Tahmures's development from capable system designer to
accountable evaluator, guardian, operator, and teacher. Its six chapters treat
quality, security, and operations as executable contracts around language—not
qualities summoned by stronger wording.

You will build anchored multi-reviewer rubrics, versioned regression gates,
source-owned trust partitions, exact approval records, segmented operational
telemetry, and a mechanism-first control selector. Every experiment is offline
and shared between Python and Go.
