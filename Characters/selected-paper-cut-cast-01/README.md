## Use the collection

1. Open `images/` for the seven individual PNGs. Each contains one complete character on the existing opaque grey background.
2. Use the image matching the character name as the visual reference. “Shahrasb/Shahrasp” refers to the same selected minister; “White Div” is Div-e Sefid.
3. Check the five new portraits against the selected board models before treating them as final identity masters. The source selections are recorded; a newly generated portrait can still differ in small facial, material or garment details. Tahmures and Akvan are unchanged byte copies and require no regeneration.
4. Keep this folder versioned. Later approved portrait refinements should use new filenames and explicit selection notes rather than overwrite these images or their source boards.

## What was prepared

- **Tahmures:** copied `Characters/Paper-Cut King in Regal Robes.png` unchanged.
- **Akvan Div:** copied `Characters/Regal Horned Forest Courtier.png` unchanged, following the owner's master-image selection. The later three-option Akvan board is not used.
- **Shahrasp:** isolated the rightmost model from the ministerial revision board, preserving the dark green formal cap and green/cream/rust court outfit.
- **Hushang:** isolated the leftmost elder royal model, preserving his oxblood robe, crown and long silver-streaked beard.
- **Black Div:** isolated the leftmost model from the original generated board, preserving ochre vest, dark warm complexion, beard and broad upward horns.
- **Div-e Sefid:** used the leftmost model from the repaired board and refined the pair of ivory horns. Both roots, continuous curves and intact tips are visible. Outfit, hair, face and build were requested unchanged.
- **Simurgh:** isolated the middle, long-necked bird, keeping the right-facing three-quarter view, long straight beak, blue/teal/gold plumage and long legs. No human form was generated.

The five individual portraits were prepared with the **built-in image_gen tool**, using the selected source boards as image references. They are new raster renders, not exact pixel crops, and contain no separately editable paper layers. No background removal or alpha cutout was requested.
